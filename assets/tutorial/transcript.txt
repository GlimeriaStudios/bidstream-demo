00:00 — Build a stronger bid, from the first decision
A strong bid starts long before the first page is written. BidStream brings opportunity discovery, company evidence and proposal preparation into one focused workspace. Follow Nordway Transport Consulting from a promising notice to a coordinated response. The dashboard keeps deadlines and next actions visible, while each pursuit brings its documents, specialists and decisions together. Start here to see what deserves attention, and where your team should move next.

00:30 — Put company evidence to work
Open the profile menu in the upper right, then choose Companies. Select Nordway Transport Consulting and open its company profile. This is where reusable bid knowledge begins. Add the business website, years in operation and contact email, then upload the company profile document. Keep the address aligned with the team handling buyer correspondence. Save the profile so that future pursuits can draw on the same company information.

01:00 — Build your reusable evidence library
Build out Nordway's evidence library with the documents a buyer may request: financial reports, project references, client testimonials, team qualifications and legal records. Choose the right document category before uploading each group. Open a file to inspect its readable content, and correct its category when needed. Keeping evidence organised now gives your team a stronger starting point when a new opportunity arrives, without rebuilding the company story every time.

01:30 — See where your profile can improve
The evidence coverage panel shows which parts of the company library are represented and where to improve next. Follow the suggested actions to add comparable projects, current registrations or relevant expert qualifications. Open the explanation to understand what strengthens an expression of interest. Then expand matching preferences and company details to refine capabilities and target markets. Save the changes, keeping both discovery and preparation grounded in a company profile.

02:00 — Bring your connected assistant into the workflow
Every company can bring its own AI workspace into BidStream. Here, Nordway uses the connected ChatGPT account for its preparation. Open Integrations from the profile menu, sign in and enable the BidStream connection in ChatGPT. Send a pursuit’s document package, work with your assistant, then return the analysis and drafts for review. Company documents and results stay organised within the right workspace, while each company chooses its own connection.

02:30 — Discover the right opportunities
Open Discover to explore opportunities from the World Bank and TED feeds. Search for a service such as road safety or transport planning, then narrow the results by market or source. Use the relevance and sorting controls to focus your review. For Nordway, the goal is a manageable set of opportunities aligned with its capabilities. Refresh sources when needed, and bring promising notices into view before preparation.

03:00 — Understand the fit and the deadline
Open a notice to understand why it matches Nordway. Review the capability and geography signals alongside the buyer's scope, then follow the source link for the original procurement details. The deadline display makes timing visible: green, amber and red are paired with clear labels and dates. Use those signals to separate work you can plan from work that needs attention, and confirm the buyer's exact closing time before proceeding.

03:30 — Shortlist in one click
When a notice deserves review, click Shortlist. The opportunity moves into Nordway's pursuit pipeline, keeping the decision connected to the notice you reviewed. Open Shortlist to compare the selected opportunities and enter the one you want to develop. This small step changes the task from scanning the market to preparing a response. Keep the shortlist focused on assignments your team has the capacity and evidence to pursue well.

04:00 — Let the dashboard guide the day
Return to Dashboard to plan your next steps. Submission and response counts sit above the next action list, where deadline and stage help prioritise each pursuit. Filter for drafting or awaiting response to focus. Open an action to continue the work in context. Direct tenders can enter the same pipeline, giving your team a route forward whether the buyer requests an initial expression of interest or a tender.

04:30 — Turn the buyer’s documents into a working brief
Inside the pursuit, upload the tender, terms of reference and supporting attachments. Choose the procurement route and add your priorities for the response. Send the document package, then open ChatGPT and request an analysis of requirements, qualification prerequisites and the buyer’s chapter structure. Return to BidStream and get the analysis and drafts. Review and apply the results to create a shared brief, with source quotations supporting each important requirement.

05:00 — Review requirements against their source
Open Requirements to inspect the extracted criteria. Read each source quotation, check the buyer's wording and confirm any supplied evaluation weight. Keep mandatory requirements distinct from scored criteria, and refine the evidence notes that will support the response. This makes the buyer's expectations visible before drafting begins. When a requirement needs clarification, resolve it against the source document so that the team works from the same interpretation and priorities.

05:30 — Close qualification gaps with confidence
Open Prerequisites to see which qualification gates need attention. BidStream suggests company evidence that may support each requirement and explains the connection. Inspect the documents and the exact threshold before changing a status to Verified. A readable financial report, for example, needs to demonstrate the requested period and capacity. Use Missing and Needs review to keep unresolved work visible, then close gaps with evidence your reviewers can stand behind.

06:00 — Coordinate specialists through your connected assistant
Open Agents to inspect the recommended specialists and drill into their assignments. Refine the brief around Nordway’s delivery approach and select the internal experts whose qualifications support the response. Send the updated package to your connected conversation and ask the assistant to coordinate the specialist contributions. Get the results back in BidStream, review each contribution and apply the work you want to carry forward into the combined response.

06:30 — Create a tailored EOI or tender response
When your evidence and specialist contributions are ready, send the updated pursuit package to the connected conversation. Ask for an expression of interest or a direct tender response, following the buyer’s structure and your company evidence. Return to BidStream, get the analysis and drafts, then review and apply the response. Open Draft to refine the wording, complete targeted inputs and sharpen Nordway’s differentiators before downloading for your reviewers.

07:00 — Organise the technical and financial response
Open Submission sheet to turn the buyer's requested structure into a response package. Review the table of contents and adjust each chapter to match the tender. The folder plan separates technical material, financial information, signed forms and correspondence, with the technical chapters nested under their own section. Download the plan to organise files consistently. See what belongs where, and keep commercial material separate while developing the technical narrative.

07:30 — Evaluate the response with your connected assistant
Send the latest pursuit package with your saved draft to the connected conversation. Ask the assistant to evaluate the response against the buyer’s criteria, explaining each rating and identifying evidence gaps. Get the assessment back in BidStream, review and apply it, then open Evaluate to inspect the findings. Challenge the reasoning, refine reviewer ratings and return to Draft to strengthen the sections that need attention before the final review.

08:00 — Bring sourced competition intelligence into the decision
In the connected conversation, ask your assistant to research comparable awards using the pursuit’s scope, buyer and location. Request direct source links and an explanation of why each comparison matters. Bring the findings back through Get analysis and drafts, then review the research and its citations before applying it. Add your assessment of positioning, partnerships and differentiation, giving Nordway a documented basis for an informed decision about this opportunity.

08:30 — Complete the submission and record the date
Return to Submission sheet for the final attachment check. Confirm the required forms, signatures and qualification evidence, then assemble the package using the chapter structure. After submitting through the buyer's required channel, open Submission and response and click Record submission. Today's date is supplied automatically, and you can adjust it to the submission date. Add the receipt or reference to keep a record of what was submitted and when.

09:00 — Keep buyer follow-up clear and timely
Review the expected response date extracted from the buyer's timetable when supplied, and check its source. If an unanswered response becomes seven days overdue, the workspace highlights the follow-up. Review the buyer address, subject and message, then copy the prepared email or open your email application. When a reply arrives, save the outcome and details. A positive EOI response moves the pursuit towards preparing the next tender stage.

09:30 — Turn opportunity into your next strong response
Nordway now has a path from relevant opportunity to carefully prepared response. Build on the evidence library, use specialist contributions to sharpen the proposal, and keep every deadline and buyer conversation in view. Return to Dashboard, open the next action and move the pursuit forward. When you expand to another company, manage its profile from the profile menu. Start with your next opportunity, and put your experience to work.
