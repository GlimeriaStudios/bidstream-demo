01:00 — Build your reusable evidence library
Build out Nordway's evidence library with the documents a buyer may request: financial reports, project references, client testimonials, team qualifications and legal records. Keeping evidence organised now gives your team a stronger starting point when a new opportunity arrives, without rebuilding the company story every time.

Then expand matching preferences and company details to refine capabilities and target markets. Save the changes, keeping both discovery and preparation grounded in a company profile.

03:30 — Shortlist in one click
When a notice deserves review, click Shortlist. The opportunity moves into Nordway's pursuit pipeline, keeping the decision connected to the notice you reviewed. Open Shortlist to compare the selected opportunities and enter the one you want to develop. This small step changes the task from scanning the market to preparing a response. Keep the shortlist focused on assignments your team has the capacity and evidence to pursue well.

04:00 — Let the dashboard guide the day
Return to Dashboard to plan your next steps. Submission and response counts sit above the next action list, where deadline and stage help prioritise each pursuit. 04:30 — Turn the buyer’s documents into a working brief
Open the pursuit overview and upload the buyer’s tender documents, terms of reference and required forms. Add your priorities in the instructions field, then share the document package with your connected assistant. Ask it to analyse the requirements using the company evidence. The assistant returns structured work directly to BidStream, where the matching fields update automatically. Open Requirements and Prerequisites to inspect the buyer’s wording and supporting evidence.

05:00 — Review requirements against their source
Open Requirements to inspect the extracted criteria. Read each source quotation, check the buyer's wording and confirm any supplied evaluation weight. Keep mandatory requirements distinct from scored criteria, and refine the evidence notes that will support the response. This makes the buyer's expectations visible before drafting begins. When a requirement needs clarification, resolve it against the source document so that the team works from the same interpretation and priorities.

05:30 — Close qualification gaps with confidence
Open Prerequisites to see which qualification gates need attention. BidStream suggests company evidence that may support each requirement and explains the connection. Inspect the documents and the exact threshold before changing a status to Verified. A readable financial report, for example, needs to demonstrate the requested period and capacity. Use Missing and Needs review to keep unresolved work visible, then close gaps with evidence your reviewers can stand behind.

06:00 — Coordinate specialists through your connected assistant
Open Agents to see the specialist assignments relevant to the pursuit. For Nordway, these include transport technical, project management, executive summary and legal work. Drill into an assignment to refine its brief and inspect the inputs. Ask your connected assistant to develop those contributions using the tender and company evidence. It saves each specialist’s work into the corresponding workspace, so your team can review individual contributions before shaping the response.

06:30 — Create a tailored EOI or tender response
Choose Automate EOI, or prepare the direct tender response, to share the current task with your assistant. In the connected company conversation, ask it to develop the response from the buyer’s requirements, your evidence and the specialist contributions. The completed text appears directly in the response editor. Refine the language, strengthen the evidence and download the draft. BidStream preserves newer edits when returned work would conflict with your changes.

07:00 — Organise the technical and financial response
Open Submission sheet to turn the buyer's requested structure into a response package. Review the table of contents and adjust each chapter to match the tender. 07:30 — Evaluate the response with your connected assistant
Share the latest pursuit package with your saved draft, then ask your connected assistant to assess it against the buyer’s criteria. Request clear reasoning for each rating and specific actions to close evidence gaps. The structured assessment populates BidStream’s evaluation workspace automatically. Open Evaluate to inspect the findings, challenge the reasoning and refine the ratings. Return to the response editor to strengthen the sections that need attention before submission.
